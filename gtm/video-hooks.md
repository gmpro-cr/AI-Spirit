# Video hook formats — faceless, AI-voiced

Ten reusable hook formats for Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts. Each clip is **30–60 seconds**, faceless, AI-voiced. Built for batch production: pick one format, swap the persona, ship 5 clips in a single session.

**Setup once (one-time, ~3 hrs):**
- Screen recorder: CapCut (free, mobile) or Descript (desktop, ~$15/mo)
- AI voice: ElevenLabs free tier for English, Sarvam.ai for Hindi/Indian languages
- Caption style: bold sans-serif, bottom-third, high-contrast box
- Outro card: 1.5 sec at end with logo + "ai-spirit.in" + "Try it free"

**Distribution per clip (10 min):**
1. Export 9:16 vertical, MP4, ≤60s
2. Upload to Instagram Reels (primary audience)
3. Cross-post to YouTube Shorts (no editing — Instagram caption works as YT title)
4. Pin a comment with: "Try it: ai-spirit.in" + emoji

**Cadence:** batch 4–5 clips Mondays (1 hr studio session), schedule across the week using Meta Business Suite.

## Format 1 — "POV: you asked X anything"

**Template:**
> POV: you opened your phone at 2 AM and asked [Persona] [question].
> [Cut to chat screen recording, AI voice reads the response]
> [End frame: persona name + ai-spirit.in]

**Example hooks (swap persona + question):**
- "POV: you asked Krishna why your situationship is breaking your heart."
- "POV: you asked Einstein how to study for an exam when your brain is fried."
- "POV: you asked Chanakya for career advice your boss won't give you."

**Why it works:** "POV" formats are algorithm-favorited. The 2 AM frame creates emotional intimacy. The question is the curiosity gap.

## Format 2 — "I asked an AI X — its answer changed my mind"

**Template:**
> I asked [Persona] [a question I thought I knew the answer to].
> Its answer was not what I expected.
> [Show full conversation, screen-record, voiceover reads both sides]
> [End frame: "Talk to [Persona] free →"]

**Example hooks:**
- "I asked an AI version of my favorite author what makes writing 'good.' Its answer was three sentences I can't stop thinking about."
- "I asked an AI version of a Stoic philosopher whether I should quit my job. It refused to answer — but the reason was the actual lesson."

**Why it works:** Reversal hooks ("not what I expected") drive completion rate, which the algorithm rewards.

## Format 4 — Side-by-side persona reactions

**Template:**
> One question. Two personas. Different worlds.
> [Split screen — left half: Persona A's chat. Right half: Persona B's chat. Same question typed in both]
> [AI voice reads both responses in sequence]
> [End: "Which one were you here for? Try it free →"]

**Example hooks:**
- "I asked Steve Jobs and Mahatma Gandhi the same question about ambition."
- "Krishna vs Buddha: what they said about heartbreak."

**Why it works:** Contrast = engagement. Comments section becomes a battle.

**Why it works:** Long-form narrative in 60 seconds = high completion + saves. Great for the algorithm.

## Production rules (apply to every clip)

1. **Hook is the first 1.5 seconds.** Caption + visual + voice all delivering the hook simultaneously. If a viewer doesn't know what the video is about by second 2, they're gone.
2. **No intro.** Skip "Hey guys, today I'm going to show you…" Get straight to the chat.
3. **Captions always.** 85% of mobile viewers watch with sound off. Captions are the video.
4. **Show the product.** Real screen recordings of real conversations beat any animation or B-roll.
5. **One CTA, end-of-clip only.** Never mid-clip. End card stays on screen for 1.5 sec.
6. **Bio link consistent.** Beacons or Linktree with "Try AI Spirit free →" as the top link. Never change it.
7. **Comments are content.** Reply to the top 5 comments on every clip within 4 hours of posting — algorithm boost is real.

## Tracking

UTM-tag the bio link: `?utm_source=ig&utm_medium=reels&utm_campaign=launch`. Track in GA4:
- Sessions from `utm_source=ig`
- Signups attributed to those sessions
- Watch time, save rate, share rate per clip (Instagram Insights)

**Kill rule:** any clip with <500 views after 48 hours = the hook didn't land. Don't try to revive it — move on. Any clip with >5,000 views = study why, repeat the formula.
