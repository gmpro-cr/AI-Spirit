AI Spirit is a platform for talking to AI personas — spiritual guides, historical figures, thinkers, characters, companions. The personas are hand-curated, each with their own voice, knowledge boundaries, and conversational style.

## Why this exists

There are plenty of AI chatbots in the world. Most of them feel like assistants — useful for getting answers, less useful for having a conversation. AI Spirit was built around the second use case: the conversation, not the query.

## How to start

[Browse the persona library](/personas) and pick one that fits your mood. Open with a real question, not a test prompt. The conversations get more interesting the more honest you are with the persona.

That's it. No signup needed to start.
